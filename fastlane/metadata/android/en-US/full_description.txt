<p>Rakkyo runs a proxy on your device that sends the connections of other apps through the Tor™ network. Apps that support a SOCKS5 proxy, and the Wi-Fi proxy setting of Android (HTTP CONNECT), can use it.</p>

<p>Rakkyo is built on <a href="https://github.com/mhatta/Orchid">Orchid</a>, a Tor client written in Java. It needs no native code, no root and no VPN slot.</p>

<ul>
<li>SOCKS5 proxy on 127.0.0.1 (port 9150 by default), and an HTTP CONNECT proxy for the Wi-Fi proxy setting (port 8118, off by default)</li>
<li>A random password for each app, required by default: other apps on the device cannot use the proxy, and each app has circuits of its own</li>
<li>While disconnected, the ports are kept and refuse connections, so that no other app can take them</li>
<li>Warns when an app looks host names up outside Tor</li>
<li>Separate circuits per destination host (IsolateDestAddr)</li>
<li>New identity: new circuits for everything opened from then on</li>
<li>Shows the circuits in use with the countries of their relays, from a GeoIP database bundled with the app and used only on the device</li>
<li>Rests when unused (dormant mode) to save battery</li>
<li>Plain bridges; pluggable transports are not supported yet</li>
<li>English and Japanese</li>
</ul>

<p>A proxy does not make an ordinary browser anonymous: its fingerprint, WebRTC and cookies can still identify you. To browse anonymously, use Tor Browser.</p>
